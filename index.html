Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and learning programs

Do I need prior programming experience to join? +

No, not at all! Our courses are designed for absolute beginners. We start with Python fundamentals and gradually progress to advanced topics. If you already have programming experience, you'll find the foundation modules quick to complete and can focus on AI-specific concepts.

What is the duration of each course? +

Course durations vary from 2 to 3 months for comprehensive programs. Most courses involve 20-30 hours per week of learning including lectures, assignments, and projects. You can work at your own pace, and we provide lifetime access to course materials.

Are certificates provided upon completion? +

Yes! Upon successful completion of any course, you receive an industry-recognized certificate. This certificate includes your name, course details, completion date, and unique verification code. Many employers value our certificates as they represent comprehensive skill development.

Do you offer payment plans or installments? +

Yes, we offer flexible payment options. You can pay the full amount upfront via UPI or bank transfer, or arrange installment plans based on your financial situation. Contact us to discuss a payment plan that works for you.

What's the job placement rate for course graduates? +

While we cannot guarantee placement, 75% of our graduates have successfully transitioned into AI/ML roles or advanced within their current organizations within 6 months of completing courses. We provide career support services including resume building, interview preparation, and industry connections.

Can I access course materials after the course ends? +

Absolutely! You get lifetime access to all course materials, lecture videos, code repositories, and resources. This allows you to revisit topics, refresh your knowledge, and stay updated as we continuously improve course content.
